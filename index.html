What is this site about?

Sanskrit in Action is an online course that teaches Sanskrit as a living language you can use. Lessons are built on the methodology of Cambridge CELTA and DELTA and follow the CEFR levels from A1 to C2. You start speaking from the first lesson. Grammar comes in as a tool for communication.

4 skills

Listening

Understand dialogues, instructions and chanted texts.

Speaking

Hold short conversations, role-plays and simple stories.

Reading

Read graded texts, from simple sentences to śāstric prose and verse.

Writing

Write messages, descriptions and paragraphs in Devanāgarī.

5 language systems

Pronunciation

Varṇamālā, Vedic accent, sandhi at word boundaries.

Grammar

Vibhakti (cases), lakāra (tenses and moods), samāsa (compounds).

Vocabulary

Themed survival fields and dhātu (verb roots) as a word-building system.

Functions

Greeting, requesting, inviting, promising, telling a story.

Discourse

Connectives (ca, tu, eva…), order of ideas, prose and verse.

Levels

A1Survival Sanskrit: pronounce the varṇamālā, introduce yourself, ask for water.
A2Sandhi in practice, the aorist, short connected dialogues.
B1Graded reading from the Hitopadeśa, retelling simple stories.
B2–C2Pāṇinian analysis, Vedic accent, verse (chandas), the Bhagavadgītā.

Why this course is different

  • Communication first. Every lesson has a real task: ask, answer, describe, tell.
  • Authentic sources. Texts from Pāṇini, the Panchatantra, the Hitopadeśa and the Bhagavadgītā, not artificial textbook sentences.
  • Learning through play. A sandhi builder, a dialogue simulator, listening quests and skill tracks.
  • Clear progress. "Can-do" goals for every level, so you always know what you are able to do.
